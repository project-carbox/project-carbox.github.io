CRYSTALLOGRAPHIC PROTOCOL • v0.4.5

Prof. Garib Murshudov's Multi-Stage Validation Protocol

Engineered by Suleiman M Hajizadeh (Institute of Molecular Biology & Western Caspian University, Baku) and Prof. Garib N Murshudov (MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Cambridge) to eliminate false-positive carboxyl contacts in the Protein Data Bank.

Stage 1: High-Resolution Geometric & Solvent Mining

Screens atomic-resolution crystal structures (≤ 1.20 Å) for side-chain Asp (OD1/OD2) and Glu (OE1/OE2) oxygen pairs within Euclidean distance d(O···O) ≤ 3.20 Å (LBHB regime: 2.40 Å ≤ d ≤ 2.55 Å). Evaluates solvent exclusion via centroid distance to nearest crystallographic water (dwater > 3.0 Å = Buried low-dielectric core, εeff ≈ 2–4).

Stage 2: Non-Parametric Tukey IQR B-Factor & Fisher Z RSCC

Applies non-parametric Tukey Interquartile Range fences (Bcutoff = Q3 + 1.5 · IQR and Q3 + 3.0 · IQR) alongside Fisher z-transformed Real-Space Correlation Coefficients (z = 0.5 · ln((1+CC)/(1-CC)), requiring ZCC ≥ -3.0 and RSCC ≥ 0.85) to discard disordered loops and misfitted side chains.

Stage 3: 70-Metal Coordination & Alternate Conformer Pruning

Excludes carboxyl pairs bridging divalent/trivalent metal cations (Zn2+, Mg2+, Ca2+, Fe2+/3+, Mn2+, Na+, K+ within 3.20 Å) and enforces crystallographic occupancy consistency (|qA − qB| ≤ 0.20, qmin ≥ 0.50).

Stage 4: Biochemical pKa Shift & REFMAC5 / Servalcat Restraints

Validates thermodynamic protonation shifts via empirical PROPKA 3 calculations (ΔpKa ≥ +2.0, local pKa ≥ 6.0–8.2) and generates asymmetric C–O (1.21 Å) / C–OH (1.31 Å) bond-length restraint dictionaries for REFMAC5 and Servalcat refinement.
